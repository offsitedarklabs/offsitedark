## What Krebs reported

[Krebs, 18 June 2026](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/06/popa-botnet-linked-to-publicly-traded-israeli-firm/). Synthient’s numbers, as Krebs states them: on the order of 1.5–2.5 million daily addresses and about 250–300 controllers. Popa runs as a plugin in the Vo1d framework, inside piracy apps including CRICFy, DooFlix, and Rapid Streamz, on unofficial Android TV boxes. Krebs links that traffic to NetNut, the proxy service of Alarum Technologies. Alarum rejected the botnet label.

The 10 June and 15 June dates in the first draft do not match the story. Krebs treats the Qurium report as same-day coverage of scraping in May, across more than 1.4 million addresses. The LG webOS and Samsung Tizen percentages are Spur’s counts of proxy SDKs in those app stores, a separate measurement. Do not file them as Popa’s smart-TV expansion.

## What happened in July

[Krebs, 2 July 2026](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/07/fbi-seizes-netnut-proxy-platform-popa-botnet/): the FBI seized NetNut domains. Google said the device pool fell by millions. Alarum’s 3 July filing reported a partial service outage.

The June daily-address range is Synthient’s figure at the time of the June story. It is not a count for November 2026. There is no CVE and no exploit repository. The apps named above are the ones in the June article. A later inventory would be a different measurement.
