## Summary

On **24–25 November 2026**, the U.S. Department of the Treasury's **Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (`FinCEN`)** posted a **BSA E-Filing Service Disruption alert** confirming an active investigation into **"unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems."** As a precaution, FinCEN isolated and took some systems offline, including **Secure Data Transfer Mode (SDTM)** batch processing.

Separately on **24 November 2026**, FinCEN issued exceptive-relief **Order `FIN-2026-R002`** granting affected filers deadline extensions through **30 December 2026** for Bank Secrecy Act (**BSA**) submissions via SDTM as well as Beneficial Ownership Information (**BOI**) reports and **FinCEN ID** updates otherwise due between **18 November and 29 December 2026**. While `FIN-2026-R002` formally cites a "service disruption" without detailing the underlying intrusion, the BSA E-Filing alert links the disruption and deadline extensions directly to the cybersecurity incident.

## Confirmed scope vs. unconfirmed details

- **Confirmed operational impact:** FinCEN stated the **BSA E-Filing website remains operational**, though certain sub-services (including SDTM) remain temporarily unavailable during isolation. The **Financial Institutions Hotline (`1-866-556-3974`)** was unaffected and remains active for expedited suspicious activity reporting.
- **Unconfirmed details:** FinCEN has not publicly disclosed the initial entry vector, affected software/CVE, intrusion start timestamp, threat actor attribution, or whether any BSA, SAR, or BOI data was accessed or exfiltrated.

## Operator actions

1. Financial institutions using SDTM and filers subject to BOI/FinCEN ID deadlines should retain documentation under **Order `FIN-2026-R002`** (30 December 2026 extension), monitor official **`fincen.gov`** and **`bsaefiling.fincen.gov`** notices for restoration updates, and use the unaffected Financial Institutions Hotline (`1-866-556-3974`) for urgent law-enforcement referrals.
2. Treat unsolicited emails or portals claiming to offer "FinCEN emergency E-Filing verification" as high-risk phishing lures while partial system outages persist.
